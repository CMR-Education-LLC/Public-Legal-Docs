COPPA Direct Notice: Spanish Montessori App

On-screen notice text

Before you create a profile for your child

To set up Spanish Montessori App for your child, we need your consent as a parent or legal guardian. Here’s exactly what that means:

What we collect about your child: - Their first name - An avatar they choose - Which lessons they complete (so they can continue from where they left off)

What we don’t collect: your child’s last name, birthdate, photo, voice, video, location, or any device advertising ID.

How we use it: only to run the app and let your child resume lessons. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Who we share it with: the service providers listed in our full Privacy Policy, only as needed to operate the app, never with advertisers or data brokers.

Your rights as a parent: you can review, export, or delete your child’s information at any time from the Parent Portal, or by emailing Cmr@cmreducate.com. Deleting your account permanently deletes your child’s profile and progress data.

By tapping “I consent,” you confirm that you are this child’s parent or legal guardian, and you agree to our full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Required separate checkbox (third-party sharing)

Per the amended COPPA Rule, third-party data sharing needs its own affirmative consent, separate from the general consent above, even where sharing is limited to service providers rather than advertisers.

Checkbox 1 (required to proceed): ☐ I am this child’s parent or legal guardian, and I consent to the collection described above.

Checkbox 2 (required to proceed, kept separate from Checkbox 1): ☐ I consent to Spanish Montessori App sharing the information above with the service providers listed in the Privacy Policy, solely to operate the app.

(Both must be affirmatively checked. Neither should be pre-checked by default. “Continue” stays disabled until both are checked.)
