Delete your Spanish Montessori App account

Spanish Montessori App · CMR Education LLC · October 30, 2026

Only a parent or legal guardian can request deletion. You do not need the app installed to make this request.

Request deletion by email. Send a message to Cmr@cmreducate.com from the email address on the parent account. Use the subject line Delete my Spanish Montessori App account and include the parent name on the account. We do not accept requests sent from any other email address.

We then send a confirmation to that same email address on the account. We delete the account only after that mailbox replies to confirm. You need access to that inbox. You do not need the app installed.

What we delete

When the account is deleted, we delete the data associated with it:

  • Parent email address, first and last name, and PIN
  • Each child profile’s first name and avatar
  • Lesson progress for those child profiles

The account is disabled when the deletion is completed, and we stop collecting data for it. Personal data is then removed from production systems within 30 days. Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 90 days, after which they are no longer kept. We do not keep this information for advertising.

You can also delete the account in the app

If you still have Spanish Montessori App installed, a signed-in parent can delete the account there:

  1. Open the Parent Portal.
  2. Choose Delete my account.
  3. Confirm the deletion.

That disables the account immediately. The same 30-day production deletion and up-to-90-day backup window apply. If you no longer have the app, use the email request above. You do not need to reinstall it.

Delete one child’s data and keep the account

If the account has more than one child profile, you can remove one profile and keep the parent account:

  • In the Parent Portal, delete that child profile, or
  • Email Cmr@cmreducate.com from the email address on the parent account. Name the child profile to delete and state that you want to keep the account. We do not accept requests sent from any other email address.

We then send a confirmation to that same email address on the account, and we delete that profile only after that mailbox replies. We then delete that child’s first name, avatar, and lesson progress. The parent email, parent name, and any other child profiles stay.

If that child is the only profile on the account, deleting the child’s information means deleting the account. Use the account-deletion request at the top of this page.

Our Privacy Policy describes the same retention rules in more detail.
