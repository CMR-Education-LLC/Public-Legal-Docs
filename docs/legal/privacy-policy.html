Privacy Policy: Spanish Montessori App

Effective date: October 13, 2026
Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Spanish Montessori App (“we,” “us,” “our”) is a Spanish-language learning app designed for children ages 4 to 8, published by CMR Education LLC. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices and rights available to parents and guardians.

Spanish Montessori App is directed to children and is designed to comply with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), as amended. If you are located in Brazil, it is also designed to comply with Brazil’s Estatuto da Criança e do Adolescente Digital (ECA Digital) and the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD).

Only a parent or legal guardian may create an account. Children do not create their own accounts, provide their own consent, or interact with any part of this app outside a profile their parent has set up for them.

2. Information we collect

2.1 From the parent or guardian (account holder)

  • Email address
  • First and last name
  • A PIN, stored as a one-way hash (we do not store your PIN in plain text)

2.2 From or about the child (within a parent-created profile)

  • First name (as entered by the parent)
  • A selected avatar
  • Lesson progress: which lessons and activities the child has completed, stored as append-only events tied to the parent’s account

We do not collect: the child’s last name, birthdate, photo, voice or video recordings, precise location, contact list, device advertising identifiers (AAID/IDFA), or any government-issued identification.

2.3 What we do not do

We do not run advertising, and we do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not build advertising profiles of children, and we do not sell personal information.

3. How we use information

Data Purpose
Parent email, name Account creation, login, account-related communication (for example, password reset or policy updates)
PIN hash Authenticating the parent when accessing the Parent Portal, so a child cannot access parental controls
Child first name, avatar Personalizing the child’s in-app experience
Progress events Letting the child continue lessons and activities from where they left off

We do not use any of the above for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating the app.

4. Verifiable parental consent

Before any information about a child is collected, the parent or guardian who creates the account must:

  1. Sign in via Sign in with Apple or Google (Amazon Cognito).
  2. Review this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service.
  3. Affirmatively confirm they are the child’s parent or legal guardian.
  4. Separately confirm consent to any third-party data sharing described in section 5, even where that sharing is limited.

Only after this consent is recorded may a parent create a child profile.

A parent may withdraw consent at any time by deleting their account from the Parent Portal (section 6), which also removes associated child profiles and progress data as described in sections 6 and 7.

5. Third-party service providers

We share limited information with the following service providers, solely to operate the app. None of these providers may use child information for their own purposes, including advertising.

Provider Purpose Data shared
Amazon Cognito (AWS) Parent authentication (Apple / Google) Parent email / OAuth identity
Vimeo Lesson video delivery (CDN) Own lesson videos. Device IP and standard request metadata when the app downloads the lesson MP4. We do not send child name, avatar, progress, or other kids PII to Vimeo.
AWS (application hosting) Application and database hosting (Lightsail / Amplify, us-east-1) All data described in section 2, as needed to operate the service

Fonts are bundled in the app binary (Nunito, Teachers, Zilla Slab). There is no runtime Google Fonts fetch. Our internal vendor inventory for operators is maintained separately and is not part of this public policy.

We do not share information with data brokers, advertisers, or analytics companies.

6. Parental rights: access, review, export, and deletion

As a parent, you may at any time, from the Parent Portal:

  • Review the child profiles associated with your account (for example, first name and avatar), and review the personal information we hold by using Export.
  • Export your account, child profiles, and progress data in a portable format.
  • Delete your account. Deletion immediately disables the account and ends further collection; associated child profiles and progress are then removed from production systems within the retention window in section 7.
  • Withdraw consent, including consent to the limited third-party sharing in section 5, by deleting your account.

You may also contact us directly at Cmr@cmreducate.com to exercise any of these rights. Steps to request deletion, including if you no longer have the app installed, are on the Delete your account page.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide the service:

  • Active accounts: retained for as long as the account exists.
  • Progress events: retained for the life of the parent account so the child can resume where they left off; removed when the account is deleted (along with child profiles).
  • After account deletion: the account is disabled immediately. Personal data is hard-deleted from production systems within 30 days. Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 90 days, after which they are no longer retained in the backup cycle we operate.

8. Data security

We maintain a written information security program (see our internal Security Program document) that includes, among other measures:

  • Encrypted credential storage (PIN stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text)
  • Encryption of data in transit
  • Access controls limiting who can access production data
  • Regular backups (AWS Lightsail automatic daily snapshots)
  • Annual review of our security practices

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children in Brazil (ECA Digital / LGPD)

If your child is located in Brazil, we process personal information in accordance with the LGPD and Brazil’s Digital ECA. You have the right to access, correct, delete, or request the portability of your child’s data, and to obtain information about the entities with which we share data. You may contact our representative for data protection matters at Cmr@cmreducate.com.

We rely only on platform-provided age signals (Apple’s Declared Age Range API, Google’s Play Age Signals API) to determine age category. We do not require Brazilian users to submit identity documents for age verification.

10. Parent PIN as a supervision tool

The Parent Portal is protected by a PIN set by the parent during account creation. This PIN is intended to help parents supervise their child’s use of the app by restricting access to account settings, data export, and deletion functions to the parent. The PIN is not a substitute for general parental supervision of the child’s device use.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, including changes to what we collect or how we share it, we will notify parents in-app and require renewed consent before the child can continue using the app, consistent with platform requirements (Apple’s Significant Change process, Google Play’s significant-change notifications).
